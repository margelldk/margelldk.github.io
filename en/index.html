House Rules

Margell D-K is a residential owners’ community in Calella de Palafrugell. So that everyone can enjoy living here, we kindly ask you to read and follow these rules. Thank you for your cooperation.

The rules in full

  1. 1

    handshakeArticle 1 Order and good neighbourliness

    The president of the community, the property administrator and the members of the board, or the persons to whom they delegate, are authorised to give appropriate instructions and to take whatever decisions are necessary to maintain order and good neighbourly relations within the community at all times.

  2. 2

    checkroomArticle 2 Drying laundry

    Hanging laundry on the outside of the communal railings or on the railings of private terraces is prohibited. Only beach towels may be hung on the railings of private terraces.

  3. 3

    local_shippingArticle 3 Traffic and parking

    Vehicles may drive across the open area in front of blocks E and G, but may not park there, except for loading and unloading.

  4. 4

    deleteArticle 4 Rubbish

    Rubbish must be put in bags intended for that purpose and placed in the containers provided by the Town Council — never in the litter bins within the community grounds or in communal areas. Nappies, old junk, cables, etc. must not be put in the litter bins either.

  5. 5

    petsArticle 5 Pets

    Pets must be kept on a lead at all times in the community’s common areas and are not allowed in the pool area under any circumstances. Owners or keepers of animals must ensure that they do not urinate or defecate in the common areas, including gardens, walkways and façades. Should this happen accidentally, the owner must immediately and hygienically pick up the faeces and pour water or disinfectant over any urine.

  6. 6

    poolArticle 6 Pool access

    Access to the pool is restricted to the owners and tenants of the apartments and their guests, provided that guests are accompanied by an occupant of the apartment and are not excessive in number. Any owner may ask people they do not know where they are staying.

  7. 7

    showerArticle 7 Pool rules

    The pool is open for swimming from 10 am to 10 pm. Showering and wearing swimwear are compulsory before entering the pool. Reserving sun loungers with towels or other items is not permitted.

  8. 8

    smoke_freeArticle 8 Pool area and smoke-free areas

    Eating is not permitted in the pool area, nor is the use of radios or music players, inflatable mattresses, balls or anything else that may disturb others. Anyone who brings chairs or similar items must take everything away when they leave. The pool, the tennis and basketball courts, the picnic area and the children’s playground are smoke-free areas.

  9. 9

    bedtimeArticle 9 Rest and noise

    Good neighbourly relations must be respected at all times of the day: please keep the volume of music systems and similar devices down and keep family gatherings quiet, especially during rest hours and between 11 pm and 8 am.

  10. 10

    sports_soccerArticle 10 Ball games

    Ball games that could be harmful to the communal surroundings or cause damage to the gardens or outdoor furniture are not permitted.

  11. 11

    pedal_bikeArticle 11 Bicycles, scooters and electric vehicles

    With the exception of young children, riding bicycles, scooters, skateboards, hoverboards or roller skates in the communal areas is not permitted; they may only be used to enter and leave the grounds, not for leisure. Electric vehicles may not be ridden under any circumstances.

  12. 12

    groupsArticle 12 Apartment occupancy

    The apartments are intended for a limited number of occupants, which must not be exceeded. This must be borne in mind particularly when letting.

  13. 13

    outdoor_grillArticle 13 Barbecues

    Barbecues are prohibited.

  14. 14

    sports_tennisArticle 14 Tennis and basketball courts

    The tennis court may be used from 9 am to 9.30 pm. In summer, each owner may use it for a maximum of half an hour per day. Sports clothing and footwear must be worn to play. The same hours apply to the basketball court.

  15. 15

    keyArticle 15 Letting

    Owners who let their apartments are responsible for ensuring that their tenants are aware of and comply with these rules. Owners are advised to display them in a visible place in the apartment.

  16. 16

    cleaning_servicesArticle 16 Upkeep of common areas

    All owners and other occupants of the apartments are responsible for the proper upkeep of the community’s common areas and services.

  17. 17

    ac_unitArticle 17 Air conditioning

    In blocks D, E, F and G, air-conditioning units must be installed at the bottom of the balcony. In blocks H, I, J and K they must also be installed at the bottom of the balcony, but if the utility area opens to the outside, they may be placed there. Installing them inside the light well is prohibited.

  18. 18

    chairArticle 18 Belongings in common areas

    In accordance with the community’s statutes and the Civil Code of Catalonia, common areas must not be used to leave personal belongings (sun loungers, chairs, bicycles and other personal items), unless the owners’ meeting has given its written consent and has expressly agreed to this.

  19. 19

    roofingArticle 19 Work on the roofs

    Any owner wishing to carry out work on the communal roofs will be liable for any damage this may cause. Before carrying out any work on the roof, they must inform the property administrator and pay a deposit of €300 into the community’s account, which will be refunded once the work has been inspected and found to be satisfactory.

  20. 20

    solar_powerArticle 20 Roof installations

    Private installations (solar panels, air-conditioning units, etc.) may not be fitted on the communal roof without the prior authorisation of the owners’ meeting.

  21. 21

    roller_shadesArticle 21 Awnings and balcony screens

    Awnings in blocks D, E, F and G must be ecru or off-white. Awnings in blocks H, I, J and K must be reference 812 INCA (beige and cream stripes). If a balcony railing is to be covered with a screen, it must be the same colour as the awning.
